A CLUB FOR THE CURIOUS

Not just pages.
Possibilities.

PROLOGUE is a student reading community at LBSITW, built around the belief that a good story can open more than a book.

We bring readers together to exchange ideas, discover unfamiliar voices, and make meaningful connections. Whether you read every day or are still searching for the book that feels like yours, you are welcome here.
